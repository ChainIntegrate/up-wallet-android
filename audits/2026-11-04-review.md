# Review of 2026-11-04: what to change in the app

The review of the release of 2026-11-03 (app 0.10.0 @ `9297142`, with the extension 0.1.9 and page 0.14.0), made
right after it by two models of different providers (Claude and Codex), as Cross_Chain's review policy says
(`AUDIT.md`). Not a professional audit. The full reports stay private until the fixes are released; then
Cross_Chain's `AUDIT.md` gets the public summary. Cross_Chain's `WALLET-BACKLOG.md` tracks this file with
those of the other repositories.

No Critical or High finding on the app. The 0.10.0 signing window (`SignerActivity`) was found to be a real
isolation boundary. `app/sign-plan.js` is shared with the extension (`ext/sign-plan.js`): its changes are
made in both, identical.
